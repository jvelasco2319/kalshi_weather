LA impossible-only paper exits and The Weather Company feed

All four TWC daily markets now read weather.com/kalshi hourly METAR and dated
Daily Climate products directly. Same station, actual published Fahrenheit,
causal capture receipts, and local-standard-time climate day; no NWS display
fallback when the TWC feed is unavailable. Model input archives remain frozen.

LA's F3, F6 and V8D paper wallets check every 30 seconds. Existing buying clocks,
weights, native limits, fee policies and saved offline replays remain unchanged.
F3's old soft probability exit is disabled in the prospective online overlay.

While the high can still rise, NO on a finite range stays possible. A certified
irreversible high floor can invalidate a finite YES or the top unbounded NO.
Ordinary preliminary observations, including a settled hourly reading, cannot
supply that certificate. They are used for monitoring only.

The new producer accepts a matching official/non-preliminary completed daily
report, after the climate day ends. It can invalidate YES outside its exact
high, or NO inside its exact high, but only if the contract is still open and
there are positive supported bids. Kalshi can accept material revisions to
these reports: they are authoritative current publications, not irrevocable.
Daily reports normally arrive after trading closes, so this feature is not a
dependable intraday rescue exit. Faster polling does not guarantee bidders.

Whole supported contracts, partial sales, native fees, five-second arrival,
shared physical depth, and proportional cost-basis removal are preserved.
No bids/missing evidence means HOLD. Sold contracts cannot settle a second time.

The saved NWS physical forecasts have not been retrained or proven equivalent
to TWC settlement. Existing target/fee admission gates remain; connecting TWC
is not permission to manufacture entries. No actual exchange orders exist.
