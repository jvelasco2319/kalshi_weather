How recovery works

Refresh market downloads missed days from the start of the test month through yesterday. Saved inputs are reused after a restart; missing inputs are retried on the next refresh. Larger gaps recover in the background.

V10 uses its unchanged weights, the day's 06 UTC HRRR and 00 UTC GEFS runs, and pressure observations available under its fixed cutoff policy. Recovery calculates the official 18 UTC daily prediction. Earlier monitoring previews are not reconstructed.

Trade estimates assume entry at the historical one-minute quote close at 18 UTC and enough contracts to buy, with fees estimated from current series settings. The actual price five seconds later and historical quantity are unknown. Missing evidence stays unavailable.

The replay account starts at $100 and uses up to 10% of available cash including fees. It waits for settlement before reusing payouts. Recovery stays separate from recorded live forecasts and the practice account. These results cannot prove what would have filled while your computer was off.
