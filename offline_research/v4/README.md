# V4 offline execution-feasibility campaign

V4 keeps the completed V3 code and evidence immutable. It imports the frozen
V3 plan language, evaluator, development gates, independent replicator, and
critic, while adding a new allocator under this directory.

The registered campaign is bounded to 12 hours, 256 epochs, 3,072 distinct
candidates, 3,300 local model calls, one local inference process, and zero paid
API dollars. Every 12-candidate epoch has fixed quotas:

- 6 interleaved execution-factorial candidates;
- 2 evidence-guided continuations or forks;
- 1 cross-model combination;
- 1 independent alternative; and
- 2 adversarial challenges.

The full design crosses four model tracks with all 3 decision times, 4 interval
widths, 4 quote ages, 4 spread limits, and 4 price bands. Its order balances
every six-candidate prefix across all three times and rotates all four levels of
every four-level execution factor. A wall-time stop therefore leaves a useful
partial design instead of another 12:00 UTC / 4 F corner.

This is the scaled Kalshi analogue of the iterative research workflow. The
execution factorial is the easier feasibility subproblem. Each epoch publishes
a compact shared evidence packet, marks a Pareto-front advance or a falsified
branch, and uses that packet for follow-up prompts and cross-model synthesis.
Resources may move among parents only inside the fixed adaptive quotas, so a
promising branch cannot erase coverage or adversarial work. Independent
replication and critic nonrejection play the role of formal verification before
any candidate can advance.

Progress is lexicographic: integrity, actual opportunity-funnel depth, at least
30 trades and settlement days, positive stress/bootstrap/fold evidence,
positive return, then the 10% return target. Merely loosening a control never
counts as progress. A positive return is only a review milestone unless every
other promotion gate passes; a champion still needs the unchanged 10% gate.
The protected final interval stays sealed, and no live or paper order is
authorized.
