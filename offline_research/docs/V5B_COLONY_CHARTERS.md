# V5B colony charters and research provenance

V5B uses four independent hypothesis populations. Each candidate carries a causal rationale, assumptions, falsification tests, data requirements, lineage, origin and stable executable fingerprint. The current proposal layer contains 22 causal seeds authored by a Codex research agent during implementation. These are agent-authored starting hypotheses, not evidence that a language model runs continuously in the background.

## Forecast probability

Mechanisms: bound raw-versus-inherited-calibrated forecast ablation, raw forecast with chronological recalibration, adjacent-bracket mass diffusion, expanding historical rank-frequency calibration, entropy abstention, modal probability separation, and conservative probability temperature. Reject mechanisms that degrade chronological calibration, depend on future labels, fail adverse-entry stress or leave too few independent dates. Calibration must use strictly preceding development outcomes; a date cannot fit its own forecast.

Research requiring additional frozen features remains blocked: HRRR/GEFS member disagreement, issue-to-issue changes, airport forecast bias and seasonal/weather-regime calibration. A frozen blended bracket probability alone cannot reconstruct these features.

## Timing and execution

Mechanisms: Grade-A-only eligibility, paid-full-book eligibility, tight spreads, adverse-entry selection and compatible combinations. Compare nested evidence restrictions on the same dates as well as their complete selected samples. Grade B+ remains weaker continuity evidence; Grade B remains an assumed-fill proxy. Neither is verified execution.

Alternative decision times, quote-age policies, order-book imbalance and partial-fill simulation require separately frozen matching-time forecasts, quote timestamps, level sizes and availability/continuity bindings. Current executable hypotheses use the existing 18:00 UTC inputs only.

## Contract and relative value

Mechanisms: interior versus tail contracts, YES versus NO asymmetry, and expected dollar profit versus relative-return selection. Examine probability mass across the complete bracket chain, but do not imply a synchronized arbitrage or multi-leg strategy when only one selected contract per day is modeled. Synchronized adjacent-bracket arbitrage and portfolios remain blocked pending a registered capital, fee and execution policy.

## Robustness adversary

Mechanisms: excluding lottery-like low prices, probability uncertainty reserves, larger expected-edge hurdles, paid-evidence-plus-reserve rules and absolute-profit selection. Falsify every promising candidate using chronological folds, best-day removal, adverse entries, evidence sensitivity and causal ablations. Explaining Fold 4 losses is development diagnosis, not an independent confirmation test. Do not directly encode the failing dates as an exclusion rule.

## Population and novelty contract

Each colony's parents are its own records. Cross-colony synthesis combines only nonconflicting nondefault changes, records both parents, and demands component ablations. A global fingerprint hashes canonical effective parameters, ignoring narrative and inactive calibration settings. Thus retitling a repeated strategy does not create new work. Equal parameter fingerprints are duplicates; equal trade selections may still emerge from distinct hypotheses and must be disclosed by the evaluator/controller.

The initial proposal API supplies four finite sensitivity epochs, with explicit `deterministic_sensitivity` provenance. Synthesis has `deterministic_cross_colony_synthesis` provenance. Neither is a fresh LLM research call. When a colony has no unique executable descendant, the proposal report requests a new agent hypothesis or stopping; it does not silently recycle proposals. Critic outputs must be persisted and evaluated by the controller. The proposal function's optional `critiques` input is currently advisory and does not claim to translate free text into code.

Additional agent records can be submitted through `validate_hypothesis`. Unrecognized executable parameters, nonfinite values, fingerprint mismatches, empty causal fields and blocked records are rejected. Adding a new feature requires an implementation and frozen input binding before registration; unsupported features cannot silently fall back to an existing parameter.

## Honest progress reporting

Report unique executable hypotheses, duplicate skips, blocked research requirements, frontier changes, successful falsifications, lineage, independent trade samples and stopped/redirection decisions. Do not equate deterministic iterations with agent reasoning calls or successful return verification. The finite initial research space can validly end without a profitable solution. Any unattended campaign must state whether new hosted/local agent reasoning actually occurred or whether it exhausted the registered seeds and descendants.
