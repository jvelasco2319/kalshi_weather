# V6.2 Corrected Forecast-First Campaign

Registered September 28, 2026. This is the experimental successor to V6.1.

V6.1 stopped before its experiment clock after independent review found two preregistration defects: its first market baseline lost bracket identifiers during scoring, and its development gates required more Grade-A days than the complete development cohort contained. The corrected baseline preserves bracket IDs and uses only paid full-book A/B+ evidence at the inherited five-second arrival. It shows market-only beating both frozen weather and a fixed half market/weather blend.

V6.2 preserves the 27-member four-colony catalog and the scientific sequence in the V6.1 goal. It changes only the scope of sample gates:

- Development forecast evaluation requires all 44 post-warmup weather dates.
- Market-relative evaluation uses the one fixed paid-book intersection and requires at least 20 paired dates.
- Development economics are diagnostic because only seven Grade-A dates exist.
- The 100-Grade-A-day and 15-per-fold requirements apply to a future one-shot confirmation, not to the 64-date development cohort.

The first wave evaluates uniform, chronological climatology, HRRR, GEFS, calibrated HRRR/GEFS, market-only, and the fixed half market/weather blend. It uses five fixed calendar folds, a circular three-day moving-block bootstrap with 10,000 resamples and seed 20260928, Brier score, and clipped log loss with epsilon 1e-12. Market comparisons never repartition after missing dates are removed.

The first wave may establish forecast skill or reject the simple blend. It cannot establish profitability. S05/S06 require deterministic 2026 observation features; M02-M04 require a prerequisite-passing weather model; M05/E04 remain blocked without registered revision snapshots. All E candidates remain diagnostic until Grade-A capacity exists. Negative controls and independent replay remain mandatory before any freeze.

V6.2 is historical and offline. It cannot read a new confirmation label, use a live feed, create a paper or live order, or reuse any May-September 2026 date as final confirmation. Its three-hour ceiling includes all later waves and never resets.
