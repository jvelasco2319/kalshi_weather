# V8 probability repair and strict replay goal

V8 asks whether the directional HRRR/GEFS signal found by V7Y can be converted into reliable bracket probabilities and whether the repaired probabilities improve the existing strict V5B Kalshi decision rule.

The forecast-development population is the already-exposed 2025 calendar study. It is development evidence and cannot become untouched confirmation. The probability colony evaluates the finite 104-member catalog registered in `v8/probability_repair_spec.json`. Candidates must eliminate zero-mass outcomes, beat frozen V7Y and uniform probability baselines on Brier score and log loss, preserve modal accuracy, and improve both scores in at least four of five chronological folds.

The fixed forecast leader is `rolling_confusion-alpha-2-w-0.75`. It retains 25% of the frozen HRRR/GEFS bracket vector and assigns 75% to an ordinal confusion distribution conditioned on the model's modal bracket. Each conditional row begins with Dirichlet alpha 2 per output bracket. During 2025 development, a prediction can use only outcomes from earlier dates. After selection, the deployable mapping is refit once on all 359 scorable 2025 dates and frozen before the 2026 replay.

The economic stage applies that exact mapping without 2026 label updates to the existing V7I July 30 through September 27, 2026 replay. It reconstructs the strict V5B NO-side choice at 18:00 UTC, requires a paid Grade-A book, and requires the same frozen order to remain executable five seconds later. Missing evidence remains an abstention. Fees and settlements use the existing bound historical rules.

The primary economic period is August 4 through September 27, 2026. These outcomes were exposed during prior research, so the result is causal stability evidence rather than independent confirmation. The research screen requires at least 30 filled dates, at least 10% aggregate return after fees, four positive chronological folds, a positive one-sided 95% moving-block-bootstrap lower bound, positive two-cent adverse-entry return, and positive return after removing the best trade. Failure of any gate is a valid result.

The regime colony tests whether simple chronological season or probability-shape regimes add stable forecast benefit. The adversarial colony checks baselines, folds, evidence grades, fees, sample size, bootstrap uncertainty, concentration, negative controls, and claim boundaries. Deterministic code owns scoring and final ranking.

V8 is offline. It cannot download current data, access a live feed, place paper or live orders, or claim that exposed historical performance confirms future profit. A promotion-grade result still requires one frozen strategy and at least 100 genuinely new Grade-A days in a one-shot confirmation.
