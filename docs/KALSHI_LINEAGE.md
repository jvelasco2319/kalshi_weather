# Lessons from the Kalshi research

This is a concise provenance guide from the original research chat and its local artifacts. It is not a fresh rerun or a claim that a trading method is confirmed. Full originals are on `swarm_vanilla_v10` and in the original research workspace.

| Stage | What changed | Lesson carried into this template |
|---|---|---|
| V1–V3 | Moved from a few recipes to more colonies, candidates and weather features | More trials alone are not a useful research loop; measurements and executable differences matter. |
| V4/V4.1 | Broader timing/calibration search and continuation after a malformed nomination | Recovery must preserve counters, deadlines and bindings. A strong small sample with assumed fills still fails acceptance. |
| V5/V5P/V5A | Focused on weather probabilities, execution evidence, fees and regime/sample robustness; acquired richer historical evidence | Missing execution evidence and sparse samples must remain visible, even when development return is positive. |
| V5B | Used actual agent reviews between epochs and stricter NO-side selection | Specific reviewed insights can guide better successors; real reasoning agents differ from deterministic populations. |
| V5B-next, friend method, V5C–F | Tested chronological variants and additional forecast methods; apparent leads did not survive all checks | Keep failed controls and reject a preferred story when its evidence fails. |
| V6 | Compared viable approaches with market baselines and adversarial checks | Better forecast accuracy does not automatically improve trade economics. |
| V7 | Expanded short positive replays toward a full-year weather study | Broader seasons and coverage can expose weaknesses hidden in small profitable periods. |
| V8 | Repaired overconfident bracket probabilities using causal rolling confusion calibration | Diagnose a measured failure, design a bounded repair, verify across earlier-only folds. |
| V9 | Froze the meteorological successor protocol | Register the next question before testing combinations. |
| V10 | Tested registered meteorological combinations; pressure-flow adjustment improved development forecast metrics | Physical hypotheses can produce useful refinements; combination search still needs multiplicity control and new confirmation. |

V8 and V10 used registered finite candidate catalogs derived from previous research findings. They were not an always-on service in which agents invented unrestricted new algorithms every iteration. The generic template makes the real agent review/synthesis loop explicit and adds hourly reflection.

The V10 forecast improvement is not a verified sustainable return or an online trading authorization. Market execution, fees, trade selection and untouched confirmation remain separate questions. The generic branch contains none of the original paid/raw datasets or live order mechanisms.
