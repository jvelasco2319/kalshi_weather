# New research problem worksheet

Fill this before changing the example configuration. Record unknown capabilities as unknown; do not launch work that depends on them. Record ordinary operational assumptions and continue independent tasks.

## Target and evidence

- Short title and exact question:
- Research mode: computational, mathematics, literature, empirical or mixed:
- Definitions, quantifiers, conditions and assumptions:
- Main formulations and easier questions, including explicit transfer tests:
- In-scope variants and exclusions:
- Existing work, known results, original artifacts and failed approaches:
- Evidence sources, versions, exact locators, access limits and protected final evidence:
- Novelty: required, optional or not required:

## Acceptance and outputs

- Observable target success, including whether a valid negative answer can satisfy the question:
- Required domain checks, rubric, thresholds, sample/uncertainty policy and accepted scope:
- Acceptance authority:
- Verification and repair authority, and the actual permission boundary protecting them:
- Deliverables and reproducibility record:
- Unresolved objections and human/domain checkpoints:

## Execution

- Actual host, models and versions, tools, permissions, storage and limitations:
- Topology and dependency structure; why parallel work is useful:
- Total active task/call policy, root slots, explorer slots and delegation depth:
- Task cost bounds, provider usage reporting, budget units, cap type and overshoot response:
- Separate research, verification and reporting allocations:
- Absolute duration/deadline, shutdown and final-report time reserve:
- Lease duration, retry policy, provider cancellation/stop receipts and recovery procedure:
- Useful-progress definition, stagnation window and one discriminating replan before retirement:
- Contract revision authority and successor-registration rule:

Prefer the simplest useful topology. An ordered proof often needs one argument owner with bounded lemma tasks; independent parameterized methods may suit multiple workers. Roles can rotate, but reviewers must not certify their own candidate or family.

The configuration is the enforceable local contract. The prompt communicates it. A prompt alone cannot create tools, protect datasets or meter provider spending.
